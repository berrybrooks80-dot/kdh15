Dohyun plays the right side of the defence pair. His recorded career spans three association seasons and five clubs, moving from house-league hockey in the Aquaz and Solar Bears programmes into the Suwon Eagles system, then into the Gyeonggi provincial select squad and finally up to U15 middle-school competition.

The 2024–25 cycle is the clearest window into his level: a club title at the Eagles Grand Festival, selection to the Gyeonggi U12 representative team, a silver medal at the 106th National Winter Games, an unbeaten run to the U13 Division AA title at the 25th Bell Capital Cup in Ottawa, and gold at the 107th National Winter Games weeks later.

Tournament record

Bell Capital Cup · U13 Division AA

Ottawa, Canada — 29 to 31 December 2025. Gyeonggi AA Blue, the provincial U12 selection. The team came through seven games unbeaten, winning six and drawing one, and took the final 4–3 against Carinthian Eagles of Austria.

7 GP · 2 G · 3 A · 5 PTS — tied 16th in tournament scoring.

The result followed the 2024 edition, where the same programme went 7–0 through U13 Division A. Individual scoring for that earlier tournament was not published, so only the team result is recorded.

107th National Winter Games · U12

Gangneung, 28 February 2026. Gyeonggi Province won the final 5–2 against the Gangwon select team to take gold — the player's second consecutive national final after silver at the 106th Games in February 2025.

U15 season at Kwangwoon

Twenty-five games across the 71st National Middle School Championship, the 46th Yuhan-Cheol Cup and the Artisan Korea Cup, producing one goal and three assists. The season closed with a run to the Artisan Korea Cup final in October 2026, immediately before the transfer to Kyunghee Middle School.
